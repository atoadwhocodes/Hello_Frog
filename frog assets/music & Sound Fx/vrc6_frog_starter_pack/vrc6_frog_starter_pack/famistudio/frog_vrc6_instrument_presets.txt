VRC6 FROG STARTER INSTRUMENT RECIPES
==================================

These are plain-language starter recipes, not a locked format.
Use them as your first-pass FamiStudio instrument settings.

1) CROAK_BODY (VRC6 Saw)
- Use for: Ribbit body
- Volume feel: high at start, then quick decay
- Pitch feel: mild downward sweep
- Character: chest / body / wet croak mass

Try this behavior:
- Start strong
- Drop quickly over ~6-8 frames
- Keep sustain very short
- Avoid making it too musical

2) THROAT_EDGE (VRC6 Pulse 1)
- Use for: Ribbit attack / nasal tone
- Duty trial: 25% first, 50% second if too thin
- Volume feel: medium-high attack, quick fade
- Pitch feel: mostly stable, slight front bend allowed

3) HOP_CLICK (VRC6 Pulse 2)
- Use for: Jump
- Duty: 12.5% or 25%
- Volume feel: sharp and short
- Pitch feel: tiny upward scoop

4) LAND_SOFT (2A03 Noise)
- Use for: Land / idle shuffle texture
- Volume feel: low-medium
- Length: very short
- Character: soft contact, not harsh burst

5) BODY_THUNK (2A03 Triangle or low VRC6 support)
- Use for: Land body
- Keep very subtle
- Remove it if land becomes muddy

General note:
If a sound stops reading clearly when one layer is muted, that can be good.
It means each layer has a job.
If every layer sounds redundant, simplify.
